Questions, answered

Do you accept customised orders?

Yes. Candles, pops, gift sets and return gifts can all be customised. Message us your idea and we will confirm what is possible.

How can I place an order?

Tap Enquire Now on any product or Request a Custom Order. WhatsApp opens with your message ready. We confirm details and payment there.

How much advance notice is required?

Please enquire as early as you can. Custom and event orders need more time than ready designs. We confirm the date with you when you enquire.

Do you make bulk or event orders?

Yes. Weddings, birthdays and corporate events are welcome. Share the quantity and the date.

Can I choose colours and designs?

Yes. You can choose colours, scents, wrapping and messages. Send a reference photo if you have one.

Do you provide return gifts?

Yes. We make return gifts in small or large quantities, packed to match your theme.

How can I contact KZ Creative?

Use the WhatsApp button on this page, or find our phone, email and Instagram in the Contact section below.
